Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 29, 2026 Application: QCanvas (Android & iOS) Package ID: com.teamvesper.qcanvas

🔒 Privacy First: QCanvas operates 100% locally on your device. We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal data, scanned codes, or custom QR designs on external servers.

01 Introduction

Welcome to QCanvas ("the App"), developed and maintained by Team Vesper ("we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to respecting and safeguarding your personal privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, what device permissions we require to perform its core functions, and how your data is treated when you use QCanvas on iOS and Android devices.

02 Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, provide an email address, or log in to use QCanvas.
  • Local Data Processing: All QR code generation, styling, customization, and barcode decoding calculations take place entirely within your device's local memory.
  • On-Device Storage: Any templates, favorites, scan history, or settings saved within the App remain exclusively in your device's secure local sandbox storage.

03 Device Permissions We Use

To provide standard QR generation, customization, and scanning capabilities, QCanvas requests the following system permissions when triggered by you:

Permission Platform Purpose
Camera iOS & Android Used strictly in real-time to scan and decode QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are never recorded or sent off-device.
Photo Library / Storage iOS & Android Used only when you choose to import an image to scan from your gallery or save an exported QR code design (PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF) to your photos.
Vibration / Haptics Android & iOS Used to provide instant haptic tactile feedback upon successfully scanning a QR code.

04 Third-Party Services

QCanvas is designed to function as an offline-first tool. We do not integrate third-party ad networks, data brokers, or user profiling trackers.

When you scan a QR code that contains a web URL and choose to open it, your default web browser handles the destination URL according to that website's own privacy policy and terms.

05 Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data generated inside QCanvas is stored locally on your device:

  • You can clear your scan history and saved designs at any time from within the App settings.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing application data via your device settings permanently removes all locally stored QR codes, history, and cache.

06 Children’s Privacy

Our App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union) in compliance with COPPA and GDPR regulations.

07 Security

We leverage native iOS and Android security sandboxing to ensure your locally generated QR designs and scans are isolated from unauthorized applications.

08 Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
