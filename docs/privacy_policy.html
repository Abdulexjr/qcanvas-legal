Privacy Policy

Wall Shot Puzzle

Developer: Team Vesper Package: com.teamvesper.wallshot Effective Date: September 12, 2026

1. Overview

Team Vesper ("we", "us", or "our") built Wall Shot Puzzle as a line-clearing directional puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, storage, and handling of information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Wall Shot Puzzle does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). All gameplay progress is saved exclusively on your local device.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We believe in complete privacy. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to play the game.

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, location, or biometric data.
  • Local Game Data: Game progress (such as completed levels, stars earned, high scores, audio settings, and haptic feedback toggles) is stored strictly on your local device storage using standard app preferences. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud database.

3. Device Permissions

Wall Shot Puzzle requests only minimal permissions strictly necessary to deliver the gameplay experience:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when shooting blocks, hitting walls, or completing levels. You can disable vibration at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, media library, or phone identifiers.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The current version of Wall Shot Puzzle (v1.0.1) does not include third-party advertising SDKs or behavioural tracking tools. Should we incorporate analytics (such as crash reporting tools to improve app stability) in future releases, this Privacy Policy will be promptly updated and will adhere to all applicable privacy regulations.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Wall Shot Puzzle is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted by the game, parents and guardians can feel confident that their children's privacy is respected.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all gameplay data is stored strictly locally on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • You can clear all saved data at any time by going to your device's Settings > Apps > Wall Shot Puzzle > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the app will automatically and permanently delete all local game data from your device.

7. Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal information over the internet, your data is not susceptible to remote server interception or data breaches on our end. We rely on the security features provided by your device's operating system (Android / iOS) to protect local application files.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
